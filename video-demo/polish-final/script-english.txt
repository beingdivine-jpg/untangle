PRODUCTION CONTEXT
Maya is fictional. The captured walkthrough, approval, guided instruction, learning feedback and recorded uncertain result are working local product interactions. The AI architecture reflects the implemented optional guide-retrieval agent in server/coach.ts. No live model answer or real volunteer conversation is presented as recorded evidence. The film shows the human-support entry point, not a promise of an available volunteer.

The opening uses the requested idea of lingering digital connections and risk. It deliberately does not claim that this is everyone's single greatest risk.
