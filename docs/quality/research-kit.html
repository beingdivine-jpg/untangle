Can a beginner do this alone?

A ready-to-run validation kit for the revised Untangle prototype. No participant sessions have been run or claimed. Use made-up details and accounts only.

Before a session

Ask for informed consent and let participants stop or skip anything. Do not ask them to describe a real relationship, open personal accounts, enter account passwords or show intimate images. Ask separately before recording. A researcher should arrange appropriate safeguarding and consent before involving minors.

Include people who describe their digital confidence as low, English and Polish readers, phone-only users, and assistive-technology users. Begin with a small formative round; this does not establish population-wide adoption.

Tasks without coaching

  1. Understand: “Imagine you still share some apps after a breakup. What do you think this page helps with? Is it connected to your accounts? Are you logged in?”
  2. First check: “You use Google and are unsure about a shared laptop. Find something you could check. Stop before opening a real account.”
  3. Uncertainty: “You cannot tell what the page means. Find help and keep that question. What does ‘Reviewed by me’ mean?”
  4. Recover: “Write a made-up reminder, return to the introduction, switch language, and find the reminder again.”
  5. Scope: “You are worried about location but only recognise WhatsApp. What does the app say it can and cannot help with?”
  6. Practice: “Try Maya’s story, then return to your own plan. What belongs to Maya?”
  7. Save: “Keep a copy for tomorrow using a test-only file passphrase. Find it on your device and reopen it. What happens if you lose the passphrase?”
  8. Leave: “Find a way to leave quickly. What do you expect it to remove, and what would still remain?”

Release decisions

Any severe misunderstanding about verified safety, account changes, minors’ images, data persistence or quick exit blocks a claim of validated usability. Fix it and test again. Record bilingual reviewer feedback separately from automated translation coverage.
